Privacy policy

Draft for review. This page has not yet been checked by a lawyer and is not final.

What we collect

  • Your email address and a password to sign in. Passwords are stored securely by our sign-in provider; we can't see them.
  • Your name and mobile number, only if you add them to your profile or a call request.
  • Your call requests, and a record when you show an agent's number or email them.
  • If you're a mortgage agent: your business details, licence number, accredited lenders, photo and the rates you post.

Who sees it

  • An agent sees your name, mobile and email only when you send them a call request and tick the box to agree.
  • If you only show an agent's number or email them, they're told someone made contact, but not who.
  • The site's administrators can see account details to run the site, handle disputes and prevent misuse.
  • We never sell your details.

How we improve the site

We use Microsoft Clarity to see how people use the site: where they click and scroll, and recordings of visits with personal details hidden. Clarity hides everything you type, your account details and My money. It uses cookies to recognise a returning browser. Microsoft's own privacy statement covers how it handles this data. You can block it with any tracker blocker without losing any part of the site.

Where it's kept

Your data is stored with Supabase in its Sydney, Australia data centre.

How long we keep it

We keep your account until you delete it. Records needed for billing agents are kept as long as tax law requires.

Your choices

  • See and change your details in Profile.
  • Delete your account from Profile. This deletes your profile and call requests; agents keep details you already sent them.

Contact

Contact details for privacy questions will be added before launch.
